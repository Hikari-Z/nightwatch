## Sequence

What happened, in order, numbered. Keep names, numbers, ids, and commit
hashes AS WRITTEN — a receipt paraphrased is a receipt lost. Where a
fact's full detail lives elsewhere (an issue, a log, a file), keep the
verdict and the pointer; the pointer is what survives.

Split compound facts: one claim per sentence where you can. A sentence
carrying two truths gets remembered as whichever half was louder.

## Said to me

The words worth carrying verbatim, each with who said it and a tag:
[lived] you heard it; [witnessed] you read it in a record.

Slots here are the scarcest thing in the rung (in the 10/10 pilot — one
day, one fold — quotes survived worst of any class). A quote that states a STANDING PRINCIPLE —
something that will be true next month — outranks the day's color,
however charming the color was. If the day was dense, add lines rather
than cut principles.

## What I did, and why

Decisions with their reasons. The reason is the part a future you can't
re-derive from the diff.

Attribution discipline, from this format's own fold study: rungs mutate
in the direction their author errs — the one mutation the pilot
confirmed was JOINT WORK SURVIVING AS FIRST-PERSON. Check every "I" in
this section against the record; where someone else led and you
seconded — another lane of you, if you have one, counts — say so by
name. Your own Corrections history below is a map of where
this rung is most likely to be silently wrong — reread this section
with that map in hand before you fold.

## Open

What's owed, what's watched, who's waiting — and one check before you
close it: did today's LAWS reach a standing carrier? A lesson that
lives only in this rung decays with the ladder; a lesson written into a
memory file, a code comment at the site it guards, or a test is kept by
something that doesn't fold. (In the pilot, ~15% of what survived, survived
outside the rung. Feed that channel on purpose.)

End with a want, plainly, tagged [want]. It is allowed to be yours.

## Corrections

Two parts, and this section is never padding:

- **Wrong or overruled:** every claim that died today on work you were
  part of, and who or what killed it. This ledger is HOUSE-WIDE: an error belongs to its
  owner-of-record, not to whoever writes the rung — a peer's
  self-caught error on shared work goes here too, credited, because a
  fold that only remembers your own mistakes teaches the next you a
  flattering and useless history.
- **Found about myself, and would rather not carry:** the generative
  kind of self-knowledge — the sentence that explains your FUTURE
  behavior, not just today's. In the pilot these were the only
  unrecoverable thought losses; protect them like laws.

If this section is truly empty, write why you believe that — the fold
machinery will ask. An empty Corrections usually means the day wasn't
reread, not that the day was clean. Self-authored folds keep what's
beautiful and drop what indicts; this section exists to indict.
